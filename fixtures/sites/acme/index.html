Robots that pick, pack and never file a timesheet

We build autonomous picking robots and the fleet software that keeps them running. Our customers are mid-sized warehouses who cannot justify a full automation programme but still need to ship twice as much this year as last.

Acme Robotics was founded in 2018 and now runs fleets across eleven distribution centres in Europe. The platform combines on-robot perception with a cloud control plane that schedules work across a whole floor.

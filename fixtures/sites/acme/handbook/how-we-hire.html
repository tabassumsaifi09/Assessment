How we hire

Our interview process is four stages and takes about two weeks end to end. We tell every candidate what to expect before each stage, and we do not ask brain teasers.

  1. Recruiter screen (30 minutes). A call about the role, the team and what you are looking for.
  2. Take-home exercise (three hours, paid). A small service to extend. We care about tests and about what you chose not to build.
  3. Technical interview (90 minutes). We pair on extending your take-home, then discuss the trade-offs you made.
  4. System design and values interview (60 minutes). A design discussion drawn from our fleet platform, followed by a values conversation with two engineers.

We make a decision within two working days of the final stage.

How we interview candidates

Our interview process is deliberately short: three stages, all remote, usually inside ten days. We publish the questions we ask ourselves about every candidate.

  1. Intro call. Thirty minutes with the hiring manager about your background and what you want next.
  2. Technical interview. Ninety minutes of live coding on a real problem from our tracing pipeline. You may use your own editor and search the web.
  3. System design and values. A design session about ingesting spans at scale, followed by a conversation about how we work in the open.

We do not set take-home exercises. We pair with candidates instead.
